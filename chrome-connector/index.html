Use your remembered IITB login

First open the official placement blog in this same Chrome profile. If IITB shows your remembered account, select it there and wait for the announcements to appear.

This one-time connector sends only secure IITB login cookies to your private Google monitor. These cookies can grant access to your IITB account. It does not collect passwords, phone OTPs, saved passwords, or other browsing data.

Connect also asks Google to verify this session and activate checks every 30 minutes, email reminders, and calendar updates. These run on Google with Chrome closed. IITB may require you to reconnect when the session expires.
