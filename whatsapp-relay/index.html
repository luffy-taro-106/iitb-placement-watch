Placement WhatsApp alerts

Link your separate sending account once, then choose receiving numbers, existing groups, or both. Your Google monitor keeps checking every 30 minutes.

Enter your private setup key to continue.

1. Link the sending account

This uses an unofficial WhatsApp linked-device connection. It stores device login keys in your private database and can send placement alerts from this account. Use your separate account. It does not display or collect your chats.

On the sending phone, open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device. Scan the QR yourself. You can revoke this device there at any time.
QR not working? Link with a phone code

Enter the number of the separate sending account. This uses WhatsApp’s “Link with phone number” option. The number is used for this linking step and is not saved here.

2. Choose recipients

Free hosting can sleep between alerts and take about a minute to wake. A revoked WhatsApp device, service outage or exhausted free allowance can require attention. Existing email and Calendar alerts continue independently.
